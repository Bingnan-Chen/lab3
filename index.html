01 / LIGHT

Why the sky looks blue

Sunlight contains many colours. As it passes through the atmosphere, shorter blue wavelengths scatter more strongly than most other visible colours. That scattered light reaches our eyes from across the sky, making the sky appear blue during much of the day.

02 / SOUND

How sound travels

Sound begins with a vibration. The vibration moves through air as a wave until it reaches the ear. Sound can also travel through liquids and solids because their particles pass the vibration along.

03 / WEATHER

How clouds form

Warm air can carry water vapour upward. As the air rises and cools, the vapour condenses into tiny droplets or ice crystals. Large groups of these droplets and crystals become the clouds we see.
